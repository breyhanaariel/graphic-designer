# 🍒 Cherry Static

### **Integrated Campaign · Advertising · Social · Art Direction · Motion**

**Independent Concept Project**

Cherry Static is a fictional independent music and arts festival built to demonstrate a high-energy campaign system across posters, outdoor, paid/social media, event graphics, merchandise, and kinetic typography.

> **Build status:** Current assets are composition and format scaffolds only. Final typography, collage/image-making, illustration, photography, merchandise, print craft, and motion will be replaced with Brianna's manually created artwork.

![Cherry Static poster scaffold](./source/poster-18x24.svg)

---

## ⚡ The Brief

Create a campaign for a two-night independent music and arts festival that feels **raw, current, expressive, and highly recognizable at speed**.

Unlike the soft consumer world of Peach & Petal or the structured B2B system of RibbonHQ, this case study should prove range through a more experimental art direction.

### Audience

- Music and arts audiences roughly 18–35
- Local creative communities
- Design-aware festivalgoers
- Social-first event discovery audiences

### Campaign challenge

The identity needs to survive:

- A crowded poster wall
- A fast social scroll
- Tiny paid-ad placements
- Giant outdoor applications
- Motion without losing legibility
- Merchandise people would actually want to keep

---

## 🍒 Creative Direction

**Art-direction keywords:** electric · editorial · imperfect · loud · nocturnal · tactile

The scaffold uses black, warm white, hot cherry, and acid-lime with oversized condensed typography and collision-based layouts. It is intentionally far from the portfolio wrapper aesthetic.

### Final exploration goals

- Original collage, photography, illustration, or mixed-media artwork
- Distinctive typographic composition
- Physical texture: scan, photocopy, marker, ink, paper, print, or handmade marks
- A modular system that changes without losing campaign recognition
- Motion that feels native to the static compositions
- Clear accessibility/legibility decisions despite expressive styling

## 🧠 Case Study Story

### 01 — Cultural Context
Define the fictional event, audience, visual territory, and what would make it distinctive among other festival campaigns.

### 02 — Handmade Exploration
Show sketches, type tests, scans, collage fragments, photography experiments, print textures, and failed compositions.

### 03 — Campaign System
Explain the rules that make highly varied assets still feel related: type scale, alignment, recurring shapes, color behavior, and image treatment.

### 04 — Poster as Hero
Use the primary poster to establish the strongest expression of the art direction.

### 05 — Adaptation
Show how the same system responds to square, 4:5, 9:16, billboard, ticket, signage, and merchandise formats.

### 06 — Paid Variants
Create 2–3 versions with different hooks while preserving campaign recognition.

### 07 — Motion
Translate the visual grammar into timing, scale changes, collisions, flicker, reveal, texture, and sound-aware pacing.

### 08 — Production + Reflection
Discuss legibility, hierarchy, print constraints, motion readability, and what changed during testing.
