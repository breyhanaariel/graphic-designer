# 🎀 RibbonHQ

### **B2B Visual Communications · Corporate Marketing · Editorial · Presentations · Motion**

**Independent Concept Project**

RibbonHQ is a fictional collaborative-workspace platform created to demonstrate the kind of multi-channel visual communication handled by an in-house or agency graphic designer supporting a growing B2B company.

> **Build status:** Current vector assets are layout and system scaffolds. Final identity, imagery, presentation design, editorial work, diagrams, templates, and motion will be replaced with Brianna's manually created work.

![RibbonHQ launch key visual scaffold](./source/launch-key-visual.svg)

---

## 💻 The Brief

Create a flexible visual-communications system for a collaboration platform launching a new feature called **Flowline**—a shared planning space that connects briefs, feedback, approvals, and handoff.

The campaign should feel **clear, capable, modern, and human** without becoming visually generic or overly playful.

### Audience

- Creative operations leads
- Marketing teams
- Small-to-mid-size agencies
- Product and project managers
- Decision-makers comparing collaboration tools

### Business communication challenge

The visual system needs to work across:

- Product-launch campaign
- Sales and enablement materials
- Executive-facing presentation slides
- Report/editorial layouts
- LinkedIn and email
- Webinar/event promotion
- Reusable internal templates
- Motion explainers

---

## 🧵 Creative Direction

**Art-direction keywords:** connected · structured · intelligent · fluid · precise · optimistic

The scaffold explores a ribbon/line motif that can connect information, frame content, become diagrams, and animate naturally. Final artwork should establish an authored B2B design voice rather than imitate a real SaaS company.

### Final exploration goals

- Refine or replace the ribbon metaphor after concept exploration
- Build a rigorous grid and type hierarchy
- Create a meaningful icon/diagram language
- Develop a campaign device that works in static and motion
- Demonstrate sophisticated information design
- Show restraint and hierarchy rather than decorating every surface

---

## 📣 Campaign Scenario

**Product:** RibbonHQ Flowline  
**Launch thought:** *Keep every idea moving.*

Flowline connects the messy middle of creative work: briefs, feedback, approvals, files, and handoff.

### Suggested messaging set

- **Keep every idea moving.**
- From brief to final without losing the thread.
- One connected view for creative work.
- Feedback, approvals, and handoff in one flow.

Use these only as scaffold copy; rewrite as the final concept develops.

## 🧠 Case Study Story

### 01 — Communication Problem
Frame the project around organizing a complex product story for several audiences, not simply “making a SaaS brand.”

### 02 — Information Hierarchy
Show message architecture, content prioritization, grid decisions, and what information belongs at each campaign touchpoint.

### 03 — Visual System
Present type, color, the ribbon/connection device, icons, diagrams, photography/illustration rules, and reusable layout logic.

### 04 — Product Launch
Show one hero campaign idea adapting to web, LinkedIn, email, webinar, and paid formats.

### 05 — Presentations
Demonstrate title slides, product explanation, comparison, data, quote/case-study, and CTA slide types.

### 06 — Editorial + Sales
Show that you can manage dense information with hierarchy through reports, one-sheets, charts, and infographics.

### 07 — Templates
Include a Canva and/or Figma template view to demonstrate realistic handoff to non-design teammates.

### 08 — Motion
Animate the system itself: lines connect, information resolves, and the story becomes easier to understand.
