## Production notes

- Build the deck on a consistent master/grid rather than treating slides as individual posters.
- Keep diagrams editable and semantically clear.
- Test dense report layouts at realistic print/viewing size.
- Make Canva templates simple enough for non-designers to safely edit.
- Export motion both as presentation-friendly MP4 and lightweight GIF where helpful.
