# Peach & Petal — Motion Brief

## Piece 1: Logo Bloom
**Length:** 3–5 seconds  
**Format:** 1080×1080 and 1080×1920  
**Goal:** introduce the identity with a tactile, organic reveal.

Suggested sequence:
1. A petal/fruit form grows or drifts into frame.
2. The wordmark resolves from mask/shape motion.
3. Secondary details settle with soft overshoot.
4. End on a clean brand lockup.

## Piece 2: Product Launch Social
**Length:** 6–10 seconds  
**Format:** 1080×1920  
**Goal:** combine product, illustration, typography, and one clear CTA.

Keep the motion simple enough to demonstrate After Effects fundamentals: masks, shape layers, easing, text animation, parented transforms, and export discipline.

The included SVG is only a lightweight scaffold. Replace it with an authored After Effects piece.
