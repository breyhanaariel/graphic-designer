FAQ

A few things clients usually want to know.

Are the listed prices final quotes?

No. They are starting prices. Final pricing depends on scope, complexity, revisions, formats, production requirements, and timeline.

Can I hire you for only one item?

Yes. A project can be as focused as one logo, mascot, flyer, poster, illustration, or presentation.

Do you provide source files?

Yes, when source files are part of the agreed deliverables. Final exports are organized for practical client use.

Can you work from an existing brand?

Yes. I can create new campaign, social, print, presentation, or motion work inside an established brand system.
